Terms & Conditions

Last updated: November 7, 2026

1. Purpose of UniFix LPU

UniFix LPU is a campus issue reporting platform designed to help students report problems related to university facilities, infrastructure and campus services.

2. Acceptable Reports

Students should submit genuine and relevant reports about problems they observe on the university campus.

  • Broken or non-working lights and electrical equipment
  • Damaged roads, pathways or infrastructure
  • Broken benches, desks or campus facilities
  • Cleanliness and waste-related problems
  • Water supply or sanitation issues
  • Other genuine campus-related problems

3. False or Misleading Reports

Users must not submit false, fake, misleading or deliberately incorrect information. Reports should describe a real issue as accurately as possible.

4. Prohibited Content

Users must not upload or submit content that is abusive, threatening, offensive, inappropriate or unrelated to the purpose of the platform.

  • No harassment of students, faculty or staff.
  • No personal disputes disguised as campus reports.
  • No intentionally offensive or inappropriate content.
  • No attempts to damage or disrupt the platform.

Important: Submitting a report does not guarantee that the issue will be immediately repaired or resolved. Reports may need to be reviewed and verified by the appropriate university authority.

5. Photos and Supporting Evidence

If users upload photographs or other supporting evidence, the content should be relevant to the reported issue. Users should avoid sharing unnecessary personal or sensitive information about other individuals.

6. Review of Reports

Submitted reports may be reviewed by the responsible team or university authority. The platform does not guarantee a particular outcome, response time or resolution for every report.

7. User Responsibility

Users are responsible for the information they provide. By submitting a report, users agree that the information provided should be accurate to the best of their knowledge.

8. Privacy

Users should provide only the information required for submitting and handling a report. Any personal information collected by the platform should be handled according to the applicable privacy practices and policies.

9. Changes to These Terms

These Terms & Conditions may be updated when necessary to improve the platform or reflect changes to its features and policies.

10. Acceptance of Terms

By using UniFix LPU and submitting a report, you acknowledge that you have read and understood these Terms & Conditions and agree to follow them.
